Global Superstore Analysis

An end-to-end statistical deep dive into a retail dataset.

Role
Team member
Type
Course team project
Focus
Statistics in Python

What it is

We took the Global Superstore retail dataset and asked it everything a statistics course could throw at it, from how the numbers are distributed to what actually predicts the outcomes.

What I did

  • Distribution fitting and maximum likelihood estimation (MLE).
  • Hypothesis testing, ANOVA and MANOVA.
  • Bayesian inference.
  • Regression: OLS, Ridge/Lasso and logistic regression.

Built with

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • NumPy
  • Statistics
  • scikit-learn
