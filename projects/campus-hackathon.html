What it is

A two-round national hackathon where we built for the place we know best: our own campus.

What I did

  • Round 1: co-developed a campus platform where students find events and join committees, study groups and research groups.
  • Round 2: co-developed a campus management platform covering the canteen, resource booking, timetables and events, with separate portals for students, faculty and admin.
