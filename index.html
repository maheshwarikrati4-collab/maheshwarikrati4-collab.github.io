About me

Hi, I'm Krati. I study Data Science in the Integrated MBA Tech programme at NMIMS (MPSTME), Mumbai, class of 2029.

Before code, there was music. I hold a diploma in Hindustani classical music from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, and right now I'm teaching myself guitar, one sore fingertip at a time. When I'm not doing either, I'm drawing: fashion sketches, zentangles, and whatever my pen decides on.

What drives me? Freedom: to build the things I want, learn whatever I'm curious about, and choose my own path.
